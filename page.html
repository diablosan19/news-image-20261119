THURSDAY 19 NOVEMBER 2026
HISTORIC

TRUMP, XI AND PUTIN: A FIRST TRILATERAL SUMMIT

The three leaders met for 90 minutes in Shenzhen and issued a four-point “Declaration Toward Peace” — UN reform, more refined fuel, limits on the ICC, and a pledge that a nuclear war “cannot be won and must never be fought.” No binding deal; Ukraine and Iran were not named in the text.

UKRAINE

KERCH BRIDGE HIT FOR A THIRD TIME IN A WEEK

Neptune missiles struck the bridge's rail section on Thursday and train traffic stopped. The road span has been shut since Tuesday's strike brought a section down.

MIDDLE EAST

MISSILE HITS RIYADH'S PIF TOWER

A suspected Houthi missile blew out windows across seven to eight floors of Saudi Arabia's sovereign wealth fund tower — the second strike on the financial district in eleven days.

JUSTICE

ICJ: MYANMAR COMMITTED GENOCIDE OF THE ROHINGYA

By 9 votes to 6, the world court found Myanmar responsible for genocide in 2017 — the first such ruling against a state — and ordered safe return and compensation for survivors.

MARKETS

ANTHROPIC: THE BIGGEST IPO IN HISTORY

The Claude maker raised a record $81.3bn at $83 a share, valuing it at about $1.5tn. Its shares start trading on the Nasdaq on Thursday under the ticker ANT.

NUCLEAR

NORTH KOREA EXPANDS YONGBYON ENRICHMENT

The IAEA says Pyongyang built an extension to its original uranium enrichment plant — externally complete by late October — and calls the programme a cause for serious concern.

ARCTIC

DENMARK RATIFIES GREENLAND DEAL, 109–0

Copenhagen unanimously approved the US security agreement for Greenland, expanding America's military role — including a Golden Dome missile shield — while keeping Danish sovereignty.
